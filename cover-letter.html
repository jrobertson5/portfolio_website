Dear Hiring Team,

I am writing to express my interest in a Software Engineer role at Sephora. As a Manager of Operations and former Human Resources Generalist within the company, I have seen how well-designed technology can improve daily work. I want to build on that experience by developing software that supports our teams and solves practical business problems.

In my current role, I have combined operational leadership with technical problem-solving. I designed and implemented an automated Purchase Request workflow using Power Automate that processed more than 200 requests and contributed to an 11% reduction in AMEX spend. I also built Power BI dashboards that help supervisors understand productivity, staffing, and cost drivers. These projects required me to translate business needs into tools that people could use in their daily work.

My experience also includes developing the DC Network Daily Update Report, which consolidated 239 daily data points across five distribution centers. Previously, as a Human Resources Generalist at Sephora, I served as the primary user acceptance tester and implementation lead for the West Distribution Center’s Eightfold platform integration. These experiences strengthened my ability to understand user needs, evaluate system behavior, and support the adoption of new technology.

Before joining Sephora, I built a new employee orientation portal at TRAX International using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. I am now pursuing a Bachelor of Science in Software Engineering at Western Governors University, with my studies in progress. My technical skills include Python, C#, SQL, HTML, and CSS, along with Git, GitHub, along with cloud and database tools. I am eager to deepen these skills through code reviews, testing, and collaboration with experienced software engineers.

I would bring firsthand knowledge of Sephora’s operations and the people who depend on its systems to an engineering team. I would bridge the gap between contextual business knowledge and technical know-how. Leading distribution center operations has taught me to clarify requirements, weigh competing priorities, and take responsibility for results. My background in human resources and organizational development also helps me communicate across teams and understand how technical decisions affect users.

I would welcome the opportunity to discuss how my internal experience, technical project work, and ongoing software engineering education could contribute to Sephora’s engineering efforts. Thank you for considering my application.
